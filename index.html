THE DETAIL THAT MATTERS

Crossing 500 changes earlier units, too.

In the reviewed May–June 2026 schedule, usage up to 500 kWh gets 200 free units. Above 500 kWh, only the first 100 are free; units 101–400 are payable at the higher-use rate. This is not one universal progressive table.
